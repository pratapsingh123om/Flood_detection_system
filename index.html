System Architecture & Methodology

Detailed breakdown of the dual-stage hybrid engineering pipeline

Phase 1

Feature Processing & Windowing

Raw meteorological variables (temperature, humidity, radiation, wind) are scaled and converted into a 7-day sliding lookback tensor ($X \in \mathbb{R}^{B imes 7 imes D}$) to preserve temporal continuity.

Phase 2

Multi-Head Attention Extraction

An Attention-LSTM extracts 32-dimensional spatial-temporal embeddings. Parallel attention heads capture immediate rainfall triggers alongside mid-week moisture accumulation.

Phase 3

Asymmetric XGBoost Regression

Extracted deep embeddings are passed to a tree-based gradient boosted regressor optimized with a custom asymmetric loss function, penalizing underpredicted peak floods 3x harder.
